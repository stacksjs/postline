# Bluesky

**You need:** your handle and an app password. No developer account.

1. In Bluesky, open **Settings > Privacy and security > App passwords** and
   create one named "Postline". To read and answer direct messages in
   Postline's inbox, tick **Allow access to your direct messages**.
2. In Postline, open **Settings > Channels > Bluesky**, enter your handle
   (`you.bsky.social`, or your own domain) and the app password, and connect.

Accounts on another server than bsky.social work too: Postline finds the
account's server from its handle. Add a second account by connecting again
with another handle.

- **Limits:** 300 characters per post, up to four images. Links get a preview
  card with its picture, unless the post has an image, which Bluesky shows
  instead.
- **Never** give Postline your account password: an app password can be
  revoked on its own at any time from the same screen.
